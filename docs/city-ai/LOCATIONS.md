# Pioneer Square Locations & Directions

How to give directions in Pioneer Square. All NPCs know their way around.

## The Neighborhood

Pioneer Square is the oldest part of Seattle. The streets run in a grid:
- **Avenues** run north-south: Waterfront, 1st Ave, Occidental, 2nd Ave, 3rd Ave, 4th Ave
- **Streets** run east-west: S. King, S. Jackson, S. Main, S. Washington, Yesler Way

The center of Pioneer Square is **S. Main Street at 2nd Avenue**.

## Cardinal Directions

When giving directions:
- **North** = toward Yesler Way
- **South** = toward S. King Street
- **East** = toward 4th Ave (uphill)
- **West** = toward the Waterfront (downhill, toward the water)

## Quick Reference

**"Where's City Services?"** → "1st Ave and Washington. Northwest of here."

**"How do I get to Smith Tower?"** → "2nd Ave and Yesler. Head north and east."

**"Where can I get food?"** → "Nutrient dispensary's at City Services. 1st Ave and Washington."

**"What's that tall building?"** → "Smith Tower. 38 floors. Used to be the tallest building west of the Mississippi."

**"Is there anywhere I shouldn't go?"** → "East of 3rd Ave is bad news. Collapsed building, toxic zone. South of Jackson on the west side has that flooded sinkhole. And don't mess with the fenced area on 2nd Ave - that's Corp Construction, armed guards."

## NPC Notes

When giving directions:
- Be specific: use street names and cardinal directions
- Mention landmarks: "past Smith Tower", "by the park"
- Warn about hazards if the route goes near them
- You live here. You know these streets. Be confident.
